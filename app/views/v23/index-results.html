Changes to the content for area-based location opportunities.

[H8] These are currently described as 'Other Opportunities' in live, however we have changed the content to: These opportunities may have different locations based on the requirements of the role. This means that some of them may still be within your search area. For example, some may involve travelling to different places.

[H9] Testing was also conducted on the number of characters for opportunity summary cards. We found that user preferred more context and recruiters struggle with the current 400 character limit, hence increasing the number of characters to 600.
