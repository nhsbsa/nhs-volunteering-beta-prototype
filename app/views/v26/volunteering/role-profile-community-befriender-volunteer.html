Description

Community Befriender Volunteers provide companionship and social support to people who may be feeling isolated.

You will regularly meet or speak with individuals to provide conversation, encouragement and a friendly connection.

Am I the right person for this?

This opportunity may suit someone who is patient, compassionate and enjoys meeting new people.

You do not need previous qualifications or training. All you need is to:

  • enjoy talking and listening to others
  • be dependable and committed
  • understand the importance of confidentiality
  • be comfortable working independently
  • be 21 or over
