Menopause and Cancer Community Support Group Session Volunteer

Information:

The organisation may close this opportunity at any time based on their needs.

Apply for this opportunity

Description

Community Support Group Session Volunteers help create a welcoming and supportive environment for people navigating menopause following a cancer diagnosis.

Working alongside an experienced group facilitator, you will help support monthly peer support sessions held at community venues across Leeds. These sessions provide a safe and welcoming space where people can connect, share experiences and support one another.

You may:

  • welcome attendees as they arrive
  • help prepare and tidy the meeting space
  • support group discussions and activities
  • help create an inclusive and respectful environment
  • follow safeguarding, confidentiality and data protection guidance
  • signpost concerns to the session facilitator where appropriate

You will not be expected to provide counselling, medical advice or clinical support.

Am I the right person for this?

This opportunity may suit someone who is compassionate, reliable and confident supporting others.

To apply, you'll need to:

  • have experience supporting people in a volunteer, community or professional setting
  • have good communication and listening skills
  • be comfortable working with people affected by cancer
  • be able to maintain confidentiality
  • be able to work sensitively with people from different backgrounds
  • be 18 or over

What happens after you apply for this opportunity

We'll get in touch with you when we review your application. This may take a few weeks.

If we'd like to take your application forward, we'll invite you to an informal conversation to discuss the role and your experience.

To become a volunteer, you may need to:

  • provide proof of identity and address
  • complete any required training
  • complete a DBS check
  • provide details of two referees
  • provide details of relevant experience
