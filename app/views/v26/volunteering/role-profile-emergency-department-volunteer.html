Description

Emergency Department Volunteers support patients and visitors within the emergency department.

You will help people find their way around the department, provide reassurance while they wait, and support staff with non-clinical tasks to improve the patient experience.

Am I the right person for this?

This opportunity may suit someone who is calm, approachable and confident speaking to a wide range of people.

You do not need previous experience, qualifications or training. All you need is to:

  • have good communication skills
  • be comfortable approaching people to offer help
  • remain calm in busy environments
  • respect patient confidentiality
  • be 18 or over
