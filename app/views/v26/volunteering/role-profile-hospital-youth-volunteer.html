Apply for this opportunity

Description

Hospital Youth Volunteers support patients, visitors and staff by helping with a range of non-clinical activities across the hospital.

You will welcome visitors, support events and activities, help people find their way around the hospital and assist staff with simple administrative tasks. This is a great opportunity to gain experience, develop confidence and make a difference in your community.

Am I the right person for this?

This opportunity may suit someone who is friendly, enthusiastic and enjoys helping others.

You do not need previous experience, qualifications or training. All you need is to:

  • have good communication skills
  • be reliable and willing to learn
  • be comfortable talking to patients and visitors
  • be able to follow instructions from staff
  • be aged 16 or over
