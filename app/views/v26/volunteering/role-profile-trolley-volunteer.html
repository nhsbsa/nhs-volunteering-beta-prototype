Information:

The organisation may close this opportunity at any time based on their needs.

Apply for this opportunity

Description

Trolley Volunteers support patients and visitors by providing refreshments, comfort items and conversation while they are in hospital.

You will visit wards with the trolley service, offering drinks and light refreshments to patients and helping create a welcoming environment.

You will also provide companionship and a friendly face to patients who may be feeling anxious, lonely or uncertain during their stay.

You may also:

  • provide conversation and companionship to patients
  • help keep the trolley stocked with refreshments and supplies
  • support patients and visitors in a polite and respectful manner
  • work alongside staff and other volunteers
  • follow confidentiality, health and safety and infection prevention guidance

Am I the right person for this?

This opportunity may suit someone who is friendly, approachable and caring.

You do not need previous experience, qualifications or training. All you need is to:

  • have good communication and listening skills
  • enjoy speaking with people from different backgrounds
  • be comfortable approaching people to offer help
  • be able to maintain confidentiality
  • be reliable and punctual
  • be 18 or over

What happens after you apply for this opportunity

We'll get in touch with you when we review your application. This may take a few weeks.

If we'd like to take your application forward, we'll invite you to an informal conversation to learn more about you and discuss the role.

To become a volunteer, you may need to:

  • provide proof of identity and address
  • complete any required training
  • complete appropriate DBS and occupational health checks
  • provide details of two referees
  • provide information about your work history
