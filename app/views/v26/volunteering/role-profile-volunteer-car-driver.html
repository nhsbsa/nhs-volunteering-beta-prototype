Apply for this opportunity

Description

Volunteer car drivers help patients attend healthcare appointments by providing transport using their own vehicle.

You will collect patients from their home and transport them to appointments and therapy groups, ensuring they arrive on time. You will provide a friendly and welcoming service and help people access the care and support they need.

You may also:

  • provide a friendly and reassuring presence during journeys
  • attend support and supervision sessions
  • complete any required training
  • maintain confidentiality at all times
  • follow health and safety, equality and data protection requirements

Am I the right person for this?

This opportunity may suit someone who is friendly, reliable and caring.

You do not need previous experience, qualifications or training.

All you need is to:

  • have good communication and listening skills
  • be confident driving independently
  • be able to treat people with dignity and respect
  • be able to maintain confidentiality
  • hold a full UK driving licence
  • have access to a suitable vehicle
  • be 18 or over

What happens after you apply for this opportunity

We'll get in touch with you when we review your application.

To become a volunteer, you may need to:

  • provide proof of identity
  • complete any required training
  • complete a DBS check
  • provide details of referees
  • complete driver and vehicle checks
  • attend support and supervision sessions during your volunteering placement
