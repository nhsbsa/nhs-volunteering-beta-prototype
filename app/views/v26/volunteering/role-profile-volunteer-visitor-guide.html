Description

Volunteer Visitor Guides help patients and visitors find their way around the hospital site.

You will welcome people arriving at the hospital, provide directions and help them reach clinics, wards and departments.

Am I the right person for this?

This opportunity may suit someone who is friendly, approachable and enjoys helping others.

You do not need previous experience, qualifications or training. All you need is to:

  • be confident speaking to members of the public
  • have good local knowledge of the hospital site
  • be able to walk around the hospital for periods of time
  • be 18 or over
  • live within 20 miles of the hospital
